## Prototype UX while shaping

A change to what learners or I see is drawn with me while we decide it, not when a worker reaches it. The grilling session draws it on the project's canvas, or starts a top-level sibling thread that does while we keep talking, and I annotate until it's right. The spec or ticket links the approved screens, and only then does it become ready; workers wire behaviour behind screens I've already seen. A worker who meets a UX question those screens don't answer sends the node back to shaping, so `desk/ATTENTION.md` doesn't fill with drawings in flight from several workers, each behind its own canvas link.

Source, 2026-11-04: "It seems the current de facto process is to make UX drawings just in time for implementation and present them to me in attention.md from the main supervision thread, and this is creating a lot of chaos. I'd rather it be that anything that would make UX changes does the prototyping interactively with me during planning, either in the same session or a sibling session from the grilling session". At the time, five of concept's six attention items were worker drawings in flight (masthead, band progress, share image, graph ground, Mission-centred runs).

Implementation: the `plan`, `shape`, `supervise` and `implement` skills; concept's `docs/design/screens.md` Process.
