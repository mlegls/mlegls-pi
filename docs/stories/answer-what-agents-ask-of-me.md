## Answer what agents ask of me

Sessions that wake while I'm away (supervisors, reconciler handlers, automations) leave what needs me in `desk/ATTENTION.md` ([[projects/mlegls-pi/guide/desk]]) and don't repeat it in chat. Today I find out by opening that file in each project, and I answer by going to the owning thread and retyping which item I mean.

I hear about a new item when it's filed → Attention in bb lists every project's items, grouped by project, with a count on the rail → I read an item where it is and write my answer under it, quoting a passage with `>` when I'm answering part of it, or take its recommendation in one click → Send delivers my answers to the threads that asked, one message per thread → an answered item stays marked as answered until its agent removes it.

Roles and reconciler status stay where they already show: the Supervise dialog and Campaigns ([[projects/mlegls-pi/stories/steer-issues-from-bb]]).
