## Manage outreach conversations

Outreach runs in hypothesis-driven waves across X DMs, Reddit DMs, LinkedIn, Substack and email (`@thread:thr_mkvvisrmtb`, `@thread:thr_d9rvn3z3te`). Today candidates and notes live in ad-hoc `.md` files scattered across scratch folders and project repos, so follow-ups slip, transcripts get pasted by hand, and kill/confirm tallies against a segment hypothesis have to be recounted from prose.

An agent adds candidates and segment hypotheses to a project's `docs/outreach/` → Outreach in the bb [tracker plugin](../../../bb-plugins/tracker/README.md) lists uncontacted leads under Candidates, active replies and due follow-ups under Queue (with a rail badge), and kill/confirm rollups under Hypotheses → I process Queue like an inbox, marking items handled as I go without waiting for sync, and click Discuss on one or more people to open a thread with their record and hypothesis pre-attached (to draft a message, critique a reply, or update their stage/verdict) → a daily bb automation syncs new messages from Beeper Desktop (on my Mac, over Tailscale) and Gmail (direct emails plus Reddit DM notification emails) into `docs/outreach/people/`, resurfacing anyone who replied or came due.
