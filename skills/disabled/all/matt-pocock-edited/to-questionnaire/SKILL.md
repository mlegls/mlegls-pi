---
name: to-questionnaire
description: "Use when a decision needs answers from someone outside the conversation."
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Grill the send, not the subject. The recipient knows something this conversation
cannot settle; the user need only supply the audience and what the answer will
let them decide.

Write `to-questionnaire-<slug>.md` with enough context for that person to answer
without this conversation. Put the decision and most important questions first;
asynchronous exchange may get only one pass. Include the deadline and expected
effort when known. Partial answers and stated uncertainty are useful.

Group by topic when it helps. A brief reason for a question earns its place
when it would otherwise invite an irrelevant answer. The result should sound
like a direct request to that person, not a form produced by an interview
framework. Returned answers feed the decision, spec, or tracked question.
