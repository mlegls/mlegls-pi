---
name: unslop
description: "Always use when writing or editing prose."
---

Remove AI patterns; keep meaning and tone. Have opinions, vary rhythm, be specific.

Beyond the usual (puffery, AI vocabulary, "not just X but Y", rule of three, hedging, chatbot phrases, sycophancy, title case, decorative emoji, curly quotes):

- No em dashes, and no parentheses or en dashes standing in for them. End the sentence or use a comma.
- Colons only before a list or example, never as a mid-sentence connector.
- Bold label + colon restating the line ("**Performance:** Performance improved") → prose. A bold lead-in ending in a period followed by new detail is fine.
- Abstract metaphor nouns → the concrete word: substrate, wedge, vector, locus, nexus, primitive (noun), harness (metaphor), surface ("API surface"), bedrock, scaffolding (metaphor), modality, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet, evacuate, endgame, north star, flywheel.
- Say what it does, not how it feels. A sentence that could appear unchanged in another project's docs says nothing about this one; cut it.
- Active voice with the actor named; passive only when the actor is unknown or irrelevant. Adverbs → a stronger verb or the number.
