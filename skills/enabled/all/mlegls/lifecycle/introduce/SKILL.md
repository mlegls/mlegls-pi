---
name: introduce
description: "Use when asked to introduce an idea or request: locate existing work, scope it, and propose the next step."
disable-model-invocation: true
argument-hint: "an idea, problem, or request"
---

1. Locate the idea in the existing project, documents, and related work.
   Establish the intended outcome, scope, and questions that affect its size.
2. Propose the useful next level:
   - Already recorded: point to the existing record. If it is current and
     planned, propose `advance` on that scope. If it has drifted from the idea
     or project state, propose triage first to reconcile it. A recorded idea
     without a plan still needs one of the paths below.
   - `map`: the destination itself is not agreed.
   - `plan`: the destination is agreed; behavior and shape are not.
   - `realize`: a sufficiently clear change, of whatever size.
3. Return the scoped understanding and recommended continuation, with the
   reason for choosing that level. Surface any choice needed to proceed.

The deliverable is a proposal to continue.
